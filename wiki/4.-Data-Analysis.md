### Delay Attribution Analysis
A root-cause breakdown of all delayed shipments ($N = 6,420$) indicates the primary sources of friction:

- **Customs & Documentation Clearance (34.2%)**: Non-standard Harmonized System (HS) codes, invoice attestation delays between Indian exporting bodies and GCC customs agencies.
- **Port Congestion & Berth Wait Time (26.8%)**: Vessel bunching during high-tide windows and holiday cycles at major regional terminals.
- **Severe Weather & Monsoon Disruptions (19.5%)**: Arabian Sea cyclones (May–June) and severe Indian monsoon rainfall (July–August) impacting feeder container movement.
- **Last-Mile / Drayage Carrier Capacity (12.1%)**: Shortage of refrigerated (reefer) chassis and bonded trucks.
- **Regulatory / Sanctions Screening (7.4%)**: Secondary inspections and cross-border physical audits.
